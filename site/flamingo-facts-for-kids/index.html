Flamingo Facts for Kids 🦩

Flamingos are bright pink birds with long legs and curvy necks. They stand on one leg, eat with their heads upside down and live in big noisy flocks. Let's wade into some fun flamingo facts!

🦩 Flamingo quick facts

Animal type
Bird
Home
Shallow lakes, lagoons and wetlands in Africa, Asia, Europe and the Americas
Food
Tiny shrimp, algae and other small water creatures
Size
About 3 to 5 feet (0.9 to 1.5 m) tall
Life span
About 20 to 30 years in the wild

25 Flamingo Facts for Kids

  1. There are six kinds of flamingos.
  2. Flamingos live in Africa, Asia, Europe, South America and the Caribbean.
  3. Flamingos aren't born pink. Their chicks are gray or white.
  4. Flamingos turn pink from pigments in the algae and tiny shrimp they eat.
  5. The more of that food they eat, the brighter pink or orange they become.
  6. Flamingos often stand on one leg, which may help them save energy and stay warm.
  7. Flamingos eat with their heads upside down.
  8. Their beaks work like a strainer to filter tiny food out of the water and mud.
  9. What looks like a flamingo's knee bending backward is really its ankle. Its knee is hidden up near its body.
  10. Flamingos have webbed feet that help them walk on soft mud.
  11. Flamingos can fly, and they often travel at night.
  12. When flamingos fly, they stretch their necks forward and their legs out behind.
  13. Flamingos live in groups called flocks, and some flocks have thousands of birds.
  14. A group of flamingos is also called a flamboyance!
  15. Flamingos 'dance' together, marching and waving their heads in big groups.
  16. Flamingo parents build nests out of mud shaped like little volcanoes.
  17. A flamingo mom usually lays just one egg at a time.
  18. Both parents take turns keeping the egg warm.
  19. Flamingo parents feed their chick a special 'milk' made in their throats.
  20. Baby flamingos have straight beaks that start to curve as they grow.
  21. The greater flamingo is the biggest kind and can be about 5 feet (1.5 m) tall.
  22. The lesser flamingo is the smallest kind but the most common.
  23. Flamingos can live in salty or very alkaline lakes where few other animals can.
  24. Flamingos honk and grunt to talk with their flock.
  25. Some flamingos in zoos have lived for more than 50 years.

Questions kids ask about flamingos

Why are flamingos pink?

Flamingos get their pink color from natural pigments in the algae and tiny shrimp they eat. Baby flamingos start out gray or white.

Why do flamingos stand on one leg?

Scientists think standing on one leg helps flamingos save energy and stay warm by keeping one leg tucked under their body.

What do flamingos eat?

Flamingos eat algae, tiny shrimp and other small creatures. They hold their heads upside down and use their beaks to strain food out of the water.

Can flamingos fly?

Yes! Flamingos are strong fliers and often fly long distances at night.
