Hey cool kids! Plooshi Music has a roaring good coloring page for you – it’s all about dinosaurs! Dinosaurs are big, amazing creatures that lived a long, long time ago. With our free dinosaur coloring page, you can make your dinosaurs look however you want. Maybe you’ll color a T-Rex green like trees, or a Stegosaurus purple like grapes. You can even make your dinosaurs have polka dots or stripes! It’s super fun because you can use your imagination to make the dinosaurs look wild and wonderful!

You can color your dinosaurs with crayons, markers, or colored pencils. And there’s more fun to be had! You can make your dinosaurs extra special with glitter glue to make them sparkle like stars, or tissue paper to give them a cool, bumpy texture. Stickers and pom poms are really fun to add too. They can make your dinosaurs look like they’re ready for a dino-party! Mixing different colors and decorations is a great way to make your dinosaurs look super cool.

Once you’re done coloring your dinosaurs, you’ll have an awesome picture. You can show it to your family and friends, and tell them all about the dinosaurs you colored. Maybe your dinosaurs are playing in a jungle or having a big dino-race! Coloring helps you learn about colors and lets you be creative, which is really important and fun.
