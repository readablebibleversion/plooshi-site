Moon Facts for Kids

Gazing up at the glowing night sky, Earth’s lone orbiting satellite captivates kids and adults alike with its allure, mystery, and natural wonder. The moon holds so many intriguing secrets spanning its craters, phases, landings, mythology and more just waiting to be uncovered. Learn all about what makes the moon such an enthralling astronomical highlight among planets with these cool facts for children.

Moon Facts for Kids FAQ

How old is the moon? Scientific theories deduce the moon likely formed 4.53 to 4.51 billion years ago early in Earth’s development about 60 to 130 million years after solar system creation.

What causes moon phases seen from Earth? The lunar phase cycle arises from variable illuminated portions that sunlight strikes as the moon orbits Earth over 27.3 days from fresh through waxing, full, waning and repeating stages.

Why does the same moon face always point toward the Earth?
The moon experiences gravitational tidal locking with Earth so identical lunar hemispheres perpetually orient planetward as it pirouettes mid orbit over 29.5 day full rotational periods creating the phenomenon.

Who was the first woman to walk on the moon? American Apollo Astronaut Sally Ride trained intensely for moonwalking but unfortunately no female stepped moon soil yet through 2022 although NASA plans targeting future manned lunar exploration inclusive commitments currently.

How many moon landings were there? In total six successful crewed NASA Apollo landings occurred between 1969 through 1972 ferrying 12 astronauts safely walking upon lunar surfaces through Apollo 11, 12, 14, 15, 16 and 17 missions each contributing valuable rock samples and photography documenting achievements extensively.
