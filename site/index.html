Questions

Is Plooshi free to watch and listen to?

Yes! All Plooshi songs and videos are free on YouTube and Spotify, and every coloring page here is a free printable PDF.

What ages are Plooshi songs for?

Plooshi is made for toddlers, preschoolers and early learners (about ages 1–6). Older siblings love singing along too.

Are the games safe for kids?

Yes. The games run right in your browser, need no sign-up, and do not collect any personal information. Videos load in YouTube privacy-enhanced mode only when you tap play.

How do I print a coloring page?

Tap the Print button on any coloring page to open the PDF, then print it at home on regular letter or A4 paper.

Can teachers use Plooshi in the classroom?

Absolutely! Our songs and printables are perfect for circle time, music class and quiet-time activities. For other uses, see our licensing page.
