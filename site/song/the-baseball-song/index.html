About this song ⚾

Baseball, baseball, America's game! Hit the ball, swing with no shame! ⚾🎶

Step up to the plate and sing along with Plooshi! "The Baseball Song" is a fun, high-energy sports song for kids that celebrates America's favorite pastime. From swinging the bat and running the bases to pitching strikeouts and hitting home runs over the fence, this song covers all the best parts of the game. Baseball, baseball, makes us proud!

This fun sports song is perfect for toddlers and preschoolers who love baseball, t-ball, and learning about sports through music. Every verse teaches a different part of the game, from hitting and catching to teamwork and cheering for your team. Homerun soaring over the fence, baseball baseball makes perfect sense!

Perfect for baseball season, little league games, t-ball practice, game day hype, sports-loving kids, P.E. class, and anytime your little one wants to feel like they're at the ballpark. Pair this with other Plooshi sports songs like The Football Song, The Sports Song, and Basketball Song for the ultimate sports songs for kids playlist!
