About this song 🇺🇸

Yo, let's learn our ABCs with a groove that moves! 🇺🇸🎶

Sing along with Plooshi and learn the alphabet through iconic American landmarks, foods, and culture! "Learn ABCs with American Pride" is a high-energy educational alphabet song for kids that takes little ones on a coast-to-coast road trip from A to Z. From apple pie and baseball to the Statue of Liberty and Yellowstone, every letter celebrates something uniquely American!

This educational music video is perfect for toddlers and preschoolers who are learning the alphabet and love all things USA. The catchy hip-hop beat and fun American-themed vocabulary make learning letters feel like a cross-country adventure. Singing and dancing all day long, A to Z, our American song!
