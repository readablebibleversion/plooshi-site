About this song 🚛

Monster truck, do do do do do do do! 🚛💨

Rev your engines and sing along with Plooshi in this high-energy monster truck song for kids! "Monster Truck" takes the catchiest sing along format ever and puts it on four giant wheels. Big wheels, mighty jumps, loud engines, muddy tracks, and going FAST. If your kid loves trucks, cars, and things that go VROOM, this song is about to be on repeat for the next month!

This fun sing along song is perfect for toddlers and preschoolers who are obsessed with monster trucks, racing, and big loud vehicles. Every verse introduces a new monster truck action with the super catchy "do do do do do do do" chorus that kids will be singing nonstop. Big big wheels, mighty jump, let's go race!

Perfect for truck-loving kids, car ride sing alongs, playtime, brain breaks, birthday parties, and anytime your little one needs a high-energy song about their favorite big machines. Pair this with other Plooshi vehicle songs like Wheels on the Bus, The Boat Song, and The Excavator Song for the ultimate vehicles playlist!
