About this song 🍓

Shake shake berry shake, watch your strawberry dance break! 🍓🎶

Get ready to shake, bounce, and dance with Plooshi in this fun, fruity sing along song! "The Strawberry Song" takes kids on a trip to the garden to pick fresh strawberries, wash them up, and do the berry dance break. Red and sweet with tiny seeds, growing close to garden weeds, pick them when they're ripe and red, that's what grandma said!

This fun fruit song for kids is perfect for toddlers and preschoolers who love food songs, garden songs, and high-energy dance breaks. Every verse teaches something new about strawberries, from how they grow on the vine to washing them before eating and making smoothies. It's a great way to get kids excited about healthy eating and nature!

Perfect for healthy eating activities, garden learning, preschool circle time, fruit themed lessons, snack time hype, and anytime your little one needs a fun reason to eat their fruits. Pair this with other Plooshi food and nature songs like Splish Splash Bath Song, The Fruits Song, The Vegetable Song, and The Flowers Song for the ultimate healthy kids playlist!
