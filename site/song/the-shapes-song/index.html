About this song 🔵

Shapes, shapes everywhere, in our world they love to share! 🔵🎶

Sing along with Plooshi and learn all about shapes! "The Shapes Song" teaches toddlers and preschoolers how to identify circles, squares, triangles, rectangles, and diamonds by counting their sides and corners. Circle's round like the sun, square has four equal sides, triangle points up so high, and rectangle stretches wide like a door. Shapes are everywhere once you start looking!

This educational shapes song for kids is perfect for little ones who are just starting to learn about geometry. Every shape comes with a fun real-world comparison that helps kids recognize shapes in the world around them. Count the sides and corners, see what shapes can do!

Perfect for preschool math learning, learning shapes for kids, classroom circle time, homeschool, and anytime your little one needs a fun way to learn geometry basics. Pair this with other Plooshi learning songs like The Shapes Rap Song, The Counting Song, The Multiplication Song, and ABC Rap Song for Kids for the ultimate kids learning playlist!
