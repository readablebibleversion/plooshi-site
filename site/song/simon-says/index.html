About this song 🤪

Simon says... get ready for the silliest game EVER! 🤪🎶

Play along with Plooshi in this hilarious, high-energy Simon Says song for kids! “Simon Says” turns the classic kids game into a nonstop dance party of silly actions, funny faces, and crazy moves. Touch your nose, stick out your tongue, wiggle like a snail, roar like a dinosaur, and freeze like a statue with spaghetti arms. Can you keep up with everything Simon says?

This fun action game song for kids is perfect for toddlers, preschoolers, and elementary-aged kids who love silly challenges and games that get them moving. Every line gives a new ridiculous action to follow, from scratching your belly with your elbow to making fish lips and crossing your eyes. The sillier you look, the better you’re doing!

Perfect for kids game time, classroom brain breaks, P.E. warmups, birthday parties, silly time at home, rainy day activities, and anytime your kids need to burn energy while laughing. Pair this with other Plooshi action songs like Head Shoulders Knees and Toes, If You’re Happy and You Know It, Do Your Ears Hang Low, and Boot Scoot Boogie Bug for the ultimate kids action song playlist!
