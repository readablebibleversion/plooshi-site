About this song 🔢

One little bird in the tree, two shiny stars that we can see! 🔢🎶

Count along with Plooshi and learn your numbers from 1 to 10! "The Counting Song" makes learning to count fun and easy for toddlers and preschoolers. Every number comes with something exciting to count, from tweeting birds and shiny stars to bright flowers, balloons, and sweet candies in a jar. Count your fingers, count your toes, count your pencils in a row!

This educational counting song for kids is perfect for little ones who are just starting to learn their numbers. The simple, repetitive lyrics and catchy melody help kids memorize numbers 1 to 10 while connecting each number to real-world objects they can see and understand. Look how good at counting we are!

Perfect for preschool math learning, learning to count, classroom circle time, homeschool, and anytime your little one needs a fun way to practice numbers 1 to 10. Pair this with other Plooshi counting and learning songs like The Ants Go Marching, Once I Caught a Fish Alive, The Multiplication Song, and The Shapes Song for the ultimate kids learning playlist!
