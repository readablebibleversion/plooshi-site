About this song 🚜

Big arms swinging left and right, digging holes with all our might! 🚜🎶

Dig along with Plooshi in this high-energy construction song for kids! "The Excavator Song" celebrates the biggest, strongest digger on the construction site. Watch the excavator scoop dirt, fill trucks with loads of clay, roll through mud and sand, and dig trenches deep and wide. Dig, dig, watch us go, up and down, high and low!

This fun vehicle song for kids is perfect for toddlers and preschoolers who are obsessed with excavators, diggers, dump trucks, and construction sites. Every verse covers a different part of what excavators do, from scooping and digging to working through sunshine and rain. If your kid loves big machines, this song is about to be on repeat!

Perfect for construction-loving kids, vehicle enthusiasts, car ride sing alongs, playtime, P.E. class, and anytime your little one wants to feel like they're operating the biggest machine on the job site. Pair this with other Plooshi vehicle songs like Wheels on the Bus, The Boat Song, Monster Truck, and Modes of Transportation Song for the ultimate vehicles playlist!

🎤 Sing together

Big arms swinging left and right 🚜
Scooping dirt up in the air 💪
Filling trucks with loads of clay 🚛
Tracks that roll through mud and sand 🏗️
Digging trenches deep and wide ⛏️
Working hard all day! 🎉
